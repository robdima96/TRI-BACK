Knowledge Base- Vector DB and Knowledge Graphs for RAG in LBP triage
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Organize vector DB into sub-collections
Allows us to apply different metadata filters, search thresholds, and LLM behavior prompts depending on the stage of the conversation.
Screening --> Discrimination --> Disposition

Subcollections
--------------

Chroma collection slugs (use with ``--sub-collection`` on ingest): ``red_flags``,
``clinical_guidelines``, ``clinical_vignettes``, ``conversation_templates``,
``diagnostic_confounders``. Human labels below map to those slugs.

Red Flags (linking symptoms and traits to red-flag diagnoses)  [Chroma: red_flags]
	e.g. "osteoporosis increases the risk of vertebral fracture"

Conversation Templates (translates colloquial patient descriptions of symptoms and medications into clinical information)  [Chroma: conversation_templates]
	e.g. "My legs feel like heavy jelly after walking 100 meters." maps semantically to neurogenic claudication

Clinical Vignettes (brief example cases with known diagnosis / triage disposition, representative across acuities)  [Chroma: clinical_vignettes]
	e.g. "A 35-year-old man presents with sudden onset of thoracic pain after lifting a heavy bag of concrete. The 
	man has no previous history of fracture and is generally in good health. He smokes 5 cigarettes a day and has done 
	so for 10 years. He is locally tender to palpation at T8 and T9 unilaterally on both sides" - green flag
	
Diagnostic Confounders (special considerations for: medications, pre-existing conditions, etc. that affect triage)  [Chroma: diagnostic_confounders]
	e.g. "Patients with Ehler-Danlos syndrome wake up at night with pain unrelated to having an inflammatory condition"
	relies on patient input matching the special condition
	pregnancy
	active cancer

Clinical Guidelines (maps data extracted from user to validated decision rules including triage priority)  [Chroma: clinical_guidelines]
	stub implementation: only two 
	--> refer urgently to care provider (red-flag diagnosis likely)
	--> manage conservatively (non-specific likely)
	Can also contain heuristic rules like the classic spinal infection triad- back pain, fever, neurological dysfunction

Chunking for relational vector database compatible with Entity and Relation Extraction (ERE) for GraphRAG
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Screening Phase
----------------
Keep query chunks nuanced and explanatory but short (300-500 tokens)
User input is first checked by safety policy (catches obvious known red flags, medical emergency (chest pain, dyspnea) and off-topic inputs)
Following signal extraction via the encoder, conversation Hx is matched against vector store sub-collections 
Each sub-collection receives a similarity score, and top-k results are pulled from each sub-collection exceeding the score threshold
Recurse standard screening questions until checklist is filled

Discrimination Phase
--------------------
When multiple parent_ids are matched, pull discriminator variables from each triggered parent
Orchestrate LLM to ask eliminating questions (highly specific red flags), e.g.:
"Do you also experience numbness around your groin [Discriminator A], or a history of recent unexplained weight loss [Discriminator B]?"
Pass back to screening phase

Disposition Phase
-----------------
Establish criteria for when a specific triage disposition can be reached
This will essentially be a process of "ruling out" all parent_id red-flag diagnoses once checklist is filled
Use standardized checklist as input to bespoke classification model
System checks disposition and requests matching chunk from Clinical Guidelines
Orchestrate LLM to read corresponding guideline text and generate the final output response 

Meta-data tags for all chunks
-----------------------------

not all fields are relevant to all chunks: "-"

chunk_id
loc (body location- all current chunks are lbp)
parent_id (points to red-flag diagnoses- for hierarchical indexing and node in GraphRAG)
evidence (source information, citation, etc.)
evidence_level (1 SRMA, 2 RCT, 3 OBS)
source_nodes (specific symptoms or traits given- for GraphRAG e.g. "numbness", "osteoporosis", "age over 50")
edges (links between source and target- for GraphRAG e.g. RISK_FACTOR_FOR, HIGHLY_SPECIFIC_FOR, MIMIC_OF)
is_specific (to mark chunks used in discriminatory questions when multiple parent_id retrieved)
is_guideline (used for entity-specific screening questions during discrimination)
