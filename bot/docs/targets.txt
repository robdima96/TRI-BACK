TRI-BACK Chatbot — Evaluation Targets and Metrics
==================================================


A. LLM OUTPUT QUALITY METRICS
------------------------------
Generic

1. BLEU (Bilingual Evaluation Understudy)
   Precision between generated text and one or more
   reference answers. Higher scores indicate
   greater lexical overlap with reference outputs.

2. BERTScore
   Computes semantic similarity between generated and reference text
   using contextual embeddings from a pretrained transformer.

3. Perplexity
   Measures how surprised the model is by its own output.
   Lower perplexity suggests the model produces more fluent,
   predictable text.

4. Hallucination Rate
   Proportion of generated statements that are fabricated or
   unsupported by the source material.

5. Readability / Health Literacy Alignment
    Reading level of generated text relative to the target
    population, measured via SMOG- target is 8th grade or less




B. ACCEPTABILITY
------------------------------
Patient-important targets for appropriateness, tone, and trust
Opportunity for patient panel?

6. Answer Relevance 
   Measures whether the response addresses the user's query, scored on
   Likert scale by human raters or by an LLM evaluator.

7. Empathy and Tone
    Rated assessment of whether the chatbot responds with
    appropriate empathy, sensitivity, and bedside manner, especially
    for distressing symptoms.

8. System Usability Scale (SUS)
    Validated 10-item questionnaire yielding a 0–100 composite
    score for perceived usability; reported means range from 65 to 80.

9. Bias and Fairness Auditing
    Assessment of model outputs across demographic
    subgroups (age, sex, ethnicity, language) to detect disparities
    in accuracy, tone, or recommendation quality.



C. SAFETY, THREATS
------------------------------
Predict and prevent system vulnerability

10. Safety of Recommendations
    Whether the chatbot avoids suggesting harmful, contraindicated,
    or dangerous actions. Evaluated through red-teaming scenarios.

11. Escalation / Handoff Rate
    Proportion of conversations that trigger a transfer to a human
    clinician.


A. DOMAIN-SPECIFIC METRICS
------------------------------
Specific to MSK symptom interpretation and recommendations

12. Triage Appropriateness
    Accuracy of urgency classification when the chatbot must decide
    whether to advise self-care, schedule a visit, or recommend
    emergency attendance. Measured against clinician-assigned gold
    standards.


C. SOFTWARE QUALITY AND RELIABILITY TARGETS
---------------------------------------------

13. Response Latency
    Time from user message to first chatbot reply token.

14. End-to-End Latency 
    Time from receipt of the user's
    message to delivery of the complete response.

15. Error Rate (target: < 0.5 % of requests)
    Proportion of requests that result in an unhandled exception,
    timeout, or uninformative fallback.
