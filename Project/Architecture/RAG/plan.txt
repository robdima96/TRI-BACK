Component: RAG Database
Source: RAG.png

Purpose:
- Retrieve authoritative clinical guidance passages for grounded generation.
- Serve tagged/vectorized evidence with strict source attribution.

Data design:
- Curated corpus from expert clinical guidelines and trusted authorities.
- Mandatory metadata schema: source authority, publication date, version, clinical domain, jurisdiction, evidence level.
- Deterministic chunking strategy to preserve clinical context and citation fidelity.
- Source freshness controls: update cadence and re-embedding SLA for revised guidance.

Retrieval and indexing:
- Tagging + vector indexing for semantic retrieval.
- Hybrid retrieval support (vector + keyword/metadata filters) for precision-sensitive questions.
- Contradiction and stale-source checks across overlapping guideline versions.

Tools:
- LangChain orchestration layer.
- Pinecone or Weaviate as vector store (implementation choice per scale/cost constraints).

Compute:
- Scales with corpus size, embedding model dimensionality, and re-index frequency.
- Budget model should include ingestion, embedding refreshes, and query-time retrieval latency.

Quality and evaluation:
- Retrieval quality metrics: precision@k, recall@k, groundedness, citation coverage.
- Clinical quality checks: recommendation consistency, contraindication detection, escalation trigger accuracy.
- Safety quality checks: irrelevant citation detection, citation mismatch detection, retrieval poisoning resilience.

Cross-cutting controls:
- Clinical safety boundaries and mandatory human handoff triggers.
- Privacy/compliance guardrails: retention limits, access controls, audit logs.
- Deployment SLOs: latency, availability, cost envelope, and incident response thresholds.
